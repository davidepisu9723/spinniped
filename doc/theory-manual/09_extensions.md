## Chapter content

This first guide deliberately stops before several important topics:

- viscous and structural damping;
- forced response to imbalance;
- anisotropic or cross-coupled bearings;
- Timoshenko shear deformation;
- distributed shaft gyroscopic effects;
- internal damping and stability;
- nonlinear bearings, rub, cracks, and other faults.

Each extension should follow the same workflow: declare the idealization and
coordinates, draw the free body, derive the balance and constitutive equations,
state boundary conditions, solve, inspect dimensions and limiting cases, and
only then compare with a numerical model.
